# Pilot Agreement — 30‑Day Outreach Pilot

This Pilot Agreement (“Agreement”) is between:
- Provider: [Your Name / Business]
- Client: [Client Name / Company]

1. Scope of Work
Provider will perform a 30‑day outreach pilot consisting of:
- Sending up to 50 personalized outreach messages (Email and/or LinkedIn) to agreed prospects.
- Manual reply handling and scheduling of qualified meetings.
- Delivery of a one‑page results report at pilot end.

2. Timeline
- Pilot term: 30 days starting on the agreed start date (which will be set after payment).
- Kickoff: 15‑minute call within 48 hours of payment.

3. Fees & Payment
- Fee: $299 due before start (PayPal/Stripe invoice).
- Optional Upgrade: $499 (Booking Guarantee) — provider will refund $100 if zero meetings are booked.
- Add-on: Prospect sourcing (50 prospects) +$99 (if requested).

4. Refund / Guarantee
- If the Booking Guarantee option is purchased and zero meetings are booked during the pilot, Provider will refund $100.
- Standard pilot ($299) has no automatic refund — Provider will work to optimize outreach and provide final report.

5. Client Responsibilities
- Provide access to calendar/contact information, and any required brand info.
- Approve prospect list or permit Provider to source prospects (if add-on selected).
- Respond to Provider queries within 48 hours to avoid delays.

6. Confidentiality & Compliance
- Provider will not sell Client data. Provider will honor opt-outs and will follow applicable anti-spam rules.
- Provider may anonymize results for marketing (e.g., “booked X meetings for a web design agency”) unless Client requests otherwise.

7. Termination
- Either party may terminate with 48 hours notice. Fees paid for completed work are non-refundable except as described in Section 4.

8. Liability
- Provider is not liable for lost opportunities, indirect damages, or deliverability issues beyond Provider’s reasonable control.
